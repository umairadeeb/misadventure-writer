# Credits

misadventure-writer is released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`).

The anti-slop rules and the drafting workflow were informed by the open-source projects below. No files were copied. The ideas were read, deduplicated, rewritten in new words, and adapted for comedy, which in several cases means deliberately reversing the original rule (for example, blanket bans on fragments, em dashes, and groups of three).

| Project | Author | License | What informed this skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) | Siqi Chen | MIT | Ranking AI tells by strength; the "mark the tells, draft, ask what still sounds AI, search again" audit loop; letting a voice sample override generic rules; never inventing facts. |
| [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop) | Hardik Pandya | MIT | Throat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches, vague declaratives, false agency, narrator-from-a-distance, binary-contrast formulas; the five-dimension self-score idea. |
| [conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing) | Conor Bronsdon | MIT | Severity tiers; patterns such as stock reaction framing, performed candor, fake-casual register, sycophancy, and manufactured punchlines; the "never inject fake personality or invented specifics" principle. |
| [nicknisi/claude-plugins](https://github.com/nicknisi/claude-plugins) (`blog-post-writer`) | Nick Nisi | MIT | Brain dump to structure workflow; "facts may come from notes, feelings may not"; interview for what the notes cannot supply; find the one turn the post exists to make; cap sustained metaphors. |
| [manojbajaj95/claude-gtm-plugin](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/claude-gtm-plugin) (`blog-writing-specialist`; formerly `gtm-skills`) | mbajaj | MIT | Concrete-over-abstract rules; mid-thought asides and fourth-wall breaks used to preempt a skeptical reader. |
| [AgriciDaniel/claude-blog](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-blog) | AgriciDaniel | MIT | Only the idea of a rubric-based self-check with a delivery threshold. None of its SEO machinery is used. |
| [sundial-org/awesome-openclaw-skills](https://github.com/sundial-org/awesome-openclaw-skills) (`blog-writer`) | sundial-org | **No license stated** | **General ideas only, no text reused:** learn voice from a folder of example posts, and save approved posts back as new examples. |
